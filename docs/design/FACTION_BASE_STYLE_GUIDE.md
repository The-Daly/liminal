# Faction Base Style Guide

This document defines the visual split between the three faction base identities. The goal is for players to recognize faction space before reading a sign.

## Shared Backrooms Rules

- All bases are built inside existing liminal architecture, not outdoors.
- Fluorescent lighting, service doors, concrete, tile, pipework, and wrong-scale rooms should remain visible.
- Bases should feel adapted to the Backrooms, not imported from a normal world.
- No faction should directly copy another game, film, wiki faction, or real-world military organization.

## M.E.G.

M.E.G. should look moderately official, organized, and safety-focused.

Visual language:

- labeled archive shelves
- map boards with pins, colored string, and clean route labels
- scanner benches and charging stations
- whiteboards, clipboards, incident tags, evidence bags
- taped hazard lines and controlled-access doors
- medical cot corner and clean emergency shelves
- modest checkpoint with posted procedure cards

Mood:

- practical
- cautious
- field-research official
- safer than the surrounding level, but still temporary

Avoid:

- luxury sci-fi labs
- perfect military bases
- giant clean command centers

## B.N.T.G.

B.N.T.G. should look moderately official, commercial, and guarded.

Visual language:

- cargo lanes and pallet racks
- marked supply shelves
- trader counters with metal grates
- ticket price boards
- contract windows
- locked cages and vault doors
- repair benches and crate scales
- visible hired guards near valuable stock

Mood:

- transactional
- organized
- opportunistic
- safer if you can pay

Avoid:

- clean corporate stores
- open-air bazaar clutter
- cartoonish gold-hoard visuals

## Clippers

Clippers should look like an indoor scrap settlement built into a Backrooms service atrium. This is the roughest, most vertical, most personal faction base.

Visual language:

- stacked scrap shacks under a fluorescent ceiling
- catwalks, ladders, suspended bridges, and crawlspace hatches
- camcorder walls and route tape
- chalk symbols and temporary Trail String anchors
- salvaged signs, lockers, sheet metal, chain-link, old carpet, and broken office panels
- central route mast or suspended signal landmark
- improvised bunks and route-runner stalls

Mood:

- lived-in
- clever
- unstable but loved
- warmer and stranger than the official groups

Avoid:

- direct Fallout/Megaton layouts, names, icons, bomb imagery, or assets
- making the base only brown/rust
- making the Clippers look like a generic raider camp

## First Playable Priority

For V0.1, only small footholds are needed:

- M.E.G.: archive office and scanner/map corner.
- B.N.T.G.: trader kiosk and guarded supply-crate pocket.
- Clippers: route wall implying the larger vertical indoor scrap settlement beyond it.

When full bases are added, preserve the current Level 1 spacing rule: major faction base entrances should not be reachable from one another in under 9 minutes by standard cautious routes.
