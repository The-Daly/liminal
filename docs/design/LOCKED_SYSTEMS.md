# Locked Systems

## Factions

| Faction | Gameplay Role | Starter Identity |
|---|---|---|
| M.E.G. | Scanner, archive, safety | Entity Scanner |
| B.N.T.G. | Trade, salvage, armor/resources | Crowbar / Salvage Kit |
| Clippers | Route-running, camcorder, hidden exits | Camcorder / Chalk Kit |

## Death

On death:
- Carried inventory is lost.
- Personal storage remains safe.
- Shared faction storage is unaffected unless a raid event targets it.
- Prototype can simply delete carried inventory.
- Later builds may spawn death caches.

## Storage

| Storage Type | Safety | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Carried | Lost on death | Backpack/gear limited |
| Personal | Safe | Capped by room tier |
| Shared | Raid-risk | Larger than personal |

## Faction Switching

Full Realm reset. Do not implement easy switching.

## NPC Recruitment

Quest-earned only. No direct NPC purchase.
