# Clippers Base Style

The Clippers base should feel like an original retro-scrap route den: lived-in, improvised, mechanically clever, and obsessed with paths.

## Direction

Use the spirit of scavenged retro survival bases without copying Fallout names, logos, assets, vault language, factions, or iconography.

## Materials

- patched sheet metal
- mismatched plywood
- old carpet strips
- salvaged signage
- wire bundles
- jury-rigged lights
- analog monitors
- VHS/camcorder shelves
- chalk route walls
- string/tape route boards
- improvised bunks

## Shapes

- cramped passageways
- stacked sleeping alcoves
- low ceilings
- crawlspace entrances
- bolted-on platforms
- route-map walls
- hidden door panels

## Color And Mood

- practical rust, faded teal, old yellow signage, dim white work lights
- not a one-note brown/orange palette
- pockets of color from chalk, tape, CRT glow, and route tags

## Gameplay Readability

- Chalk and string indicate routes, not decoration only.
- Camcorder walls imply discovered paths.
- Hidden exits should feel maintained by people who survive by knowing routes.
- The base should look clever but fragile.
