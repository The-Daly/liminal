# Clippers Base Style

The Clippers base should feel like an original indoor retro-scrap settlement: lived-in, improvised, mechanically clever, and obsessed with paths. The target feeling is an inside-the-Backrooms version of a vertical scrap town built in the shell of a service hall, with the social density and cobbled-together silhouette of classic post-apocalyptic settlements, while staying fully original to Liminal Dominion.

## Direction

Use the spirit of scavenged retro survival bases without copying Fallout names, logos, assets, vault language, factions, layouts, signs, props, or iconography.

The Clippers base should not look like a clean bunker. It should look like people found a tall, wrong-shaped service chamber and built upward, inward, and around danger because the routes told them to.

## Core Silhouette

- indoor scrap town built inside a large Backrooms service atrium
- layered catwalks and shacks bolted into concrete walls
- central vertical landmark, such as a route mast, signal pole, or suspended camcorder tower
- uneven stairs, ladders, crawlspace hatches, and rope/string guides
- clustered homes and stalls arranged around route boards instead of streets
- patched metal walls leaning at strange angles, but braced believably
- ceiling pipes, vents, and fluorescent grids visible above the settlement

## Materials

- patched sheet metal
- mismatched plywood
- old carpet strips
- salvaged signage
- wire bundles
- jury-rigged lights
- analog monitors
- VHS/camcorder shelves
- chalk route walls
- string/tape route boards
- improvised bunks
- chain-link panels
- old service doors reused as walls
- repurposed lockers and elevator gates
- broken office blinds and acoustic ceiling panels

## Shapes

- cramped passageways
- stacked sleeping alcoves
- low ceilings
- crawlspace entrances
- bolted-on platforms
- route-map walls
- hidden door panels
- vertical shack stacks
- narrow balcony rings
- suspended bridges
- tilted sheet-metal roofs under indoor ceiling tiles
- central gathering pit or route circle

## Color And Mood

- practical rust, faded teal, old yellow signage, dim white work lights
- not a one-note brown/orange palette
- pockets of color from chalk, tape, CRT glow, and route tags
- warmer than M.E.G. and messier than B.N.T.G.
- Backrooms yellow should appear as decayed wall/ceiling material, not as a clean faction color

## Gameplay Readability

- Chalk and string indicate routes, not decoration only.
- Camcorder walls imply discovered paths.
- Hidden exits should feel maintained by people who survive by knowing routes.
- The base should look clever but fragile.
- The central vertical landmark should help players reorient from multiple levels of the base.
- Security positions should be improvised lookout nests, not formal guard booths.
- Trader stalls should be built from scrap, curtains, lockers, and hand-painted route signs.

## Contrast With Other Factions

- M.E.G. bases should look more official: labeled archives, taped hazard zones, clean map boards, scanner benches, medical corners, and organized security checkpoints.
- B.N.T.G. bases should look more official-commercial: inventory counters, guarded vault doors, marked supply shelves, cargo lanes, price boards, and contract windows.
- Clippers should look least official and most inhabited: hand-built, vertical, patched, noisy, personal, and route-obsessed.
