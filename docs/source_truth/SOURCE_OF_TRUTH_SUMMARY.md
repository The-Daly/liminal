# Source-of-Truth Summary

This file condenses the uploaded PDFs into development rules Codex can apply directly.

## Master Framework

The master framework locks the core concept: hard-loss Backrooms extraction survival, PvPvE conflict, Movie Tickets, Almond Water, sanity, M.E.G., B.N.T.G., Clippers, faction reset, personal rooms, shared faction hubs, expansion bases, and Relic-Class collectibles.

## Seamless World / Storage / Raids Addendum

Locked rules:
- No conventional loading screens.
- Use diegetic transition patterns A/B/C/D.
- Personal room storage is safe but capped.
- Shared faction storage is larger and raid-risk storage.
- Faction hubs and expansion bases are raidable only when the owning faction has online presence.
- Personal rooms are never raidable.
- Movement is on foot only.

## Level Atlas Research Pack

Locked rules:
- Foundational level scope is Levels 0, 1, and 2.
- Prototype focuses on Level 1 with a Service Halls sub-section.
- Level 1 supports the hub, Trader's Vault concept, Service Halls, and Clippers hideout.
- Level 2 is the vertical-slice expansion target.
- Backrooms Wiki material must be cited and not bulk-copied.
- Specific authored names/entities should be originalized unless legally approved.

## NPC Population / Recruitment Addendum

Locked rules:
- NPCs spawn per level.
- Discovery archetypes: Trainer, Quest-Giver, Recruitable.
- Recruitment is earned through quest completion, not trade.
- Wild inventory is smaller than hub inventory.
- Recruited NPCs provide continuous quest pipelines.
- NPC quarters are capped by hub tier.
